Process

Our packaging design process is structured to ensure strategic clarity, strong shelf impact, and flawless production execution.

01

Discovery & Strategy

We begin by understanding the product, brand, and market context. This stage includes analysis of the target audience, competitive landscape, brand positioning, and technical or regulatory requirements.

Outcome — A clear strategic foundation and design direction.

02

Creative Concept Development

Based on the approved strategy, we develop creative packaging concepts that communicate the brand’s value and differentiate it in the market. This includes visual direction, moodboards, and initial design concepts.

Outcome — 3 distinct creative concepts for review and selection.

03

Packaging Design Development

Once a concept is approved, we refine it into a complete packaging design. This includes layout, typography, color system, brand elements, and all required content across front, back, and side panels.

Outcome — Finalized packaging design for 1 SKU.

04

Technical Preparation & Prepress

We create packaging for other SKU’s in line and adapt the approved design to technical specifications, including dielines, print requirements, materials, and finishing options. Files are prepared according to production standards to ensure accuracy and quality.

Outcome — Print-ready packaging files.

05

Brand Guidelines

Packaging final visuals and brand guidelines.

Outcome — Brand Guidelines and packaging images for keyvisuals.
